# Dr. Jia Yang
## Assistant Professor | Academic Researcher
**Oklahoma State University** *Department of Natural Resource Ecology and Management (NREM)*

---

## 🔬 Research Interests
I specialize in **fire ecology**, **wildfire modeling**, and **hydrologic processes**. My work focuses on:
* **Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) Fire Risk:** Modeling fire growth and resilience in the I-35 corridor.
* **Ecohydrology:** Monitoring evapotranspiration, groundwater storage, and watershed water balances using GRACE satellite data and USGS records.
* **Remote Sensing:** Utilizing MODIS, Earth Engine, and ArcGIS for fuel load recovery and fast-growing wildfire analysis.

---

## 🛠 Technical Expertise
* **Programming:** Python (pandas, arcpy, xgboost, PyTorch), Scratch, PictoBlox.
* **GIS & Modeling:** ArcGIS, Google Earth Engine, SWAT-MODFLOW, Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
* **Data Analysis:** Satellite-derived fire growth rates (2001–2024), hydrologic modeling, and machine learning.
